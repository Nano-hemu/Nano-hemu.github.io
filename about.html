Summary

Engineer first, analyst second, systems thinker throughout.

I work across electrochemical energy technologies with experience spanning materials, MEAs, stacks, pilot systems, failure analysis, manufacturing-linked development, data analytics, and techno-economic thinking. My strongest work happens when a problem is multidisciplinary: performance loss may originate in materials, interfaces, operating conditions, manufacturing variation, or data quality.

I use electrochemical diagnostics and engineering fundamentals to define the mechanism, then data pipelines, modelling, statistics, and structured root-cause methods to test the explanation. The objective is practical: stronger validation, faster failure closure, better yield, clearer scale-up decisions, and more defensible engineering evidence.
